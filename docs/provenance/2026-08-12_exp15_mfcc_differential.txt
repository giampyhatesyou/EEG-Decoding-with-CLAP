  -- WHAT MAKES IT DIVERGE (added after the first run, because the contract says the
     divergence must be SAID, and saying it usefully means naming its cause; no
     threshold, test or branch rule was touched -- Comandamenti #2) --
     The accuracy IS the fraction of POSITIVE differentials: argmax(corr) == attended
     is the same event as d_i > 0 (asserted below, exactly). Phi(D) equals that
     fraction only if d is NORMAL. So every point of divergence is non-normality of
     d, and its SIGN says which way: d skewed right => the mean is pulled above the
     median => Phi(D) OVERSTATES how often d is positive, and vice versa.
     cell            frac(d>0)  accuracy   Phi(D)     skew  exc.kurt  Phi(med/sd)

     Read the last column against the third: the MEDIAN-based figure is the one that
     tracks the accuracy, which is what a mean/sd summary of a non-normal d cannot do.
