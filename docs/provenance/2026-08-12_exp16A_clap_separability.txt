2. THE RECIPE IS FROZEN IN THE CODE, before any number exists (contract SSA.3):
        hop    62.5 ms  -> series rate 16 Hz -> Nyquist 8 Hz = the top of the band
        window 62.5 ms  = hop, consecutive, non-overlapping
        input  mono, resampled 44100 -> 48000 with scipy.signal.resample_poly(x, 160, 147)
        output 512 dims per frame, float32, one <md5-of-the-stem>.npy per stem
   Three independent reasons for that window, all written before any number:
   (a) MEASURED in laion_clap.training.data.get_audio_features: any input shorter than 10 s
       is `repeatpad`, i.e. the waveform is REPEATED floor(480000/len) times and only the
       remainder is zero-filled. 3000 samples divides 480000 exactly (160 repeats), so this
       window is padded with NO zeros at all -- the model never sees invented silence.
   (b) a sliding aperture of length W attenuates the series like |sinc(f W)|. At W = 125 ms
       the first null falls exactly on 8 Hz and the top of the pre-registered band is
       annihilated by the aperture itself; at W = 62.5 ms the first null is at 16 Hz and the
       whole 1-8 Hz band survives (0.64 at 8 Hz).
   (c) window = hop makes the frames disjoint, so no correlation between neighbouring frames
       is manufactured by overlap.
